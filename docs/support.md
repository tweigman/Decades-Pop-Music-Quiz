# Decades: Pop Music Quiz Support

Decades: Pop Music Quiz is a name-that-tune party game for iPhone and iPad, by Amberic LLC.

## How to play

1. Choose **Speak answers** or **Type answers**, then tap **Play**.
2. Say or tap how many players there are (1 to 6), then pick a category:
   a decade, or Holiday in season.
3. Listen to the clip, then name the song, the artist, or both. Naming both
   earns a bonus. Players take turns; pass the phone around.
4. Say **"repeat"** to hear the clip again, **"skip"** to pass, or
   **"exit game"** to stop. The on-screen buttons do the same.
5. After the last round, every song you heard is listed with a link to the
   full track on Apple Music.

## Troubleshooting

- **It doesn't hear me.** Allow the microphone and speech recognition in
  Settings › Decades. Speak after the clip ends, close to the phone. In a
  noisy room, start a game with **Type answers**.
- **"Can't load the songs."** Clips stream from Apple Music, so Decades needs
  an internet connection.
- **The voice sounds robotic.** Download an Enhanced or Premium voice in
  Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, then pick it in Pop
  Quiz's settings.
- **No Holiday category?** It appears from November 20 to January 6. Turn on
  **Enable extended playlists** in settings to play it any time.
