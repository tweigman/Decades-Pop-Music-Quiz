# Decades: Pop Music Quiz

A name-that-tune party game for iPhone and iPad, by Amberic LLC.

Hear a few seconds of a hit from the 1950s to today, then call out the song,
the artist, or both. Made for a group: pass the phone, play hands-free with
your voice, or type your answers in a noisy room.
