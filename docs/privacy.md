# Privacy Policy

*Pop Quiz, by Amberic LLC. Last updated: November 25, 2026.*

**Pop Quiz collects no personal information.** There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or tracking, and nothing about you or your games is sent to Amberic
LLC or anyone else.

## Your voice

When you play with spoken answers, Pop Quiz uses the microphone only while it
is waiting for an answer. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech
recognition **on your iPhone**; the audio and the text never leave the device
and are not saved. In a typed-answer game the microphone is never turned on.

## Music

Song clips are streamed from Apple Music's public song previews. To play a
clip, your iPhone requests it from Apple, as it would for any web content;
Apple's privacy policy covers those requests. Tapping a song's link opens it
in Apple Music.

## Settings

Your choices (quizmaster voice, speak or type, seasonal playlists) are stored
on your iPhone only, and are deleted if you delete the app.

## Tips

If the app offers optional tips, they are processed by Apple through the App
Store. Amberic LLC does not receive your name or payment details.

## Children

Pop Quiz does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.
