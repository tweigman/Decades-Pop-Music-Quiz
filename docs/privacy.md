# Privacy Policy

*Decades: Pop Music Quiz, by Amberic LLC. Last updated: November 27, 2026.*

**Decades: Pop Music Quiz collects no personal information.** There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or tracking, and nothing about you or your games is sent to Amberic
LLC or anyone else.

## Your voice

When you play with spoken answers, Decades uses the microphone only while it
is waiting for an answer. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech
recognition **on your device**; the audio and the text never leave the device
and are not saved. In a typed-answer game the microphone is never turned on.

## Music

Song clips are streamed from Apple Music's public song previews. To play a
clip, your device requests it from Apple, as it would for any web content;
Apple's privacy policy covers those requests. Tapping a song's link opens it
in Apple Music.

## Settings

Your choices (quizmaster voice, speak or type, seasonal playlists) are stored
on your device only, and are deleted if you delete the app.

## Tips

If the app offers optional tips, they are processed by Apple through the App
Store. Amberic LLC does not receive your name or payment details.

## Children

Decades: Pop Music Quiz does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tweigman/Decades-Pop-Music-Quiz/issues).
